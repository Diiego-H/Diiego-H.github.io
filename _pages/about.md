I am a PhD student in the [Learning and Machine Perception (LAMP)](http://lamp.cvc.uab.es/) group at the [Computer Vision Center (CVC)](https://www.cvc.uab.es/) (Barcelona), supervised by [Joost van de Weijer](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Gsw2iUEAAAAJ&hl=en). My current research focuses on **generative models**, with an earlier background in **virtual humans**.

My background sits between mathematics and computing. I hold a double Bachelor's degree in [Mathematics and Computer Science](https://web.ub.edu/en/web/facultat-matematiques-informatica/w/infograu-bachelordegree-g1166) from the [Universitat de Barcelona](https://www.ub.edu/) (2025) and a Master's in [Computer Vision](https://mcv.uab.cat/) from the [Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona](https://www.uab.cat/) (2026).

I started doing research during my studies. For my bachelor's thesis I joined the [Human Pose Recovery and Behavior Analysis (HuPBA)](https://hupba.com/) group, where I contributed to [MV-Fashion](https://hunorlaczko.github.io/MV-Fashion/) (CVPR 2026), a large multi-view video dataset for virtual try-on and size estimation. During my master's, I built the dataset for [NumColor](https://github.com/moatifbutt/numcolor/) (ECCV 2026), a method that gives text-to-image diffusion models precise control over numerical colors such as hex and RGB codes.
